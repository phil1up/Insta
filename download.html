After you tap Save to Photos

  1. Wait a few seconds while the video loads (~8MB).
  2. iPhone share sheet appears → tap Save Video.
  3. Open Photos — it should be in Recents.
  4. Instagram → Reels → pick it from your library.

If it already downloaded to Files

  1. Open the Files app.
  2. Go to Downloads (On My iPhone or iCloud Drive).
  3. Find tcg_hs_charizard_glvx_reel.mp4.
  4. Tap it → Share (box with arrow) → Save Video.

Shortcut: Instagram can also pick the file from Files when you create a Reel — you don’t always need Photos.
